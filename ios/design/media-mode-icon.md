# 沉浸浏览模式图标

- 方式：内置 `image_gen.imagegen`（imagegen 技能），透明背景；作为 iOS template image 随界面着色。
- 资源：`../Flow/Resources/Assets.xcassets/MediaMixed.imageset/media-mixed.png`。
- 用途：混合模式的图片与视频切换菜单。图片、视频单独模式使用系统 `photo` / `video` 符号。

生成提示词：

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production iOS toolbar icon asset, single monochrome UI glyph. Create one clean white outline icon representing mixed photo and video browsing: a rounded rectangular landscape photo frame with a tiny mountain and sun, overlapped at the lower right by a small rounded video tile containing a clearly cut-out triangular play symbol. Centered, symmetrical balanced optical weight, uniform bold rounded strokes, flat pure white with transparent negative spaces, genuinely transparent background. No color, no gradients, no shadows, no lettering, no UI screenshot, no border beyond the glyph. The glyph should fill approximately 80% of a square canvas and remain readable as a 24pt iOS toolbar template icon. Crisp vector-like raster edges.
